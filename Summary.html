HackInsight '26 · Data-to-Decision · results from the real-data run, model trained 2026-10-21 04:35 UTC

Problem and decision

Smog advisories assume you can stay indoors. Outdoor workers, children and their schools can't, and the US EPA guide lists outdoor workers among the groups most exposed. PeshGo forecasts the mean PM2.5 of the next 24 hours, gives a likely range, and turns it into advice in Urdu and English for outdoor workers, parents and schools, or everyone, with a message to share.

Agents, app and pipeline

Insight Agent: retrieval over WHO, US EPA AirNow and the Punjab Clean Air Action Plan; forecast numbers come only from stored rows and are shown with their row IDs; every claim is cited; false-premise questions are corrected; off-topic gets "I don't know"; Urdu in and out. Supervisor: one failing source never stops a run. Drift monitor and human approval are built in. The public app is a phone-style page (Urdu or English, share button, installable) that each pipeline run rebuilds; 3 completed hourly Prefect runs are recorded in prefect_runs.csv. Median pipeline run 178 s.

Notes

  • The forecast is the city-wide 24-hour average across Lahore's monitors, not a single street. Readings from low-cost sensors in humid air are marked, not corrected.
  • Information, not medical advice: follow Punjab government and school notices. The Urdu wording was drafted with AI help and the app says so.
